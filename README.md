## Project overview

First Pages is an online experience for personalized children's books. The portfolio case study focuses on designing a warm, gift-oriented storefront while making the path from product discovery to checkout easy to understand and trustworthy.

The project combines e-commerce UX, responsive web design, visual storytelling, content hierarchy, and WordPress/WooCommerce implementation.

## The design challenge

The main challenge was not simply making the storefront more attractive. Personalized products require shoppers to understand **what they are buying**, **how personalization works**, and **whether the checkout experience feels trustworthy**.

Areas identified for improvement included:

- **Hero messaging:** Make the product understandable immediately, without asking the visitor to decode broad marketing language.
- **Trust and credibility:** Present a polished, consistent experience before requesting personal or payment information.
- **Checkout friction:** Reduce uncertainty around product selection, personalization, shipping, and checkout.
- **Mobile hierarchy:** Keep critical information and calls to action easy to find on smaller screens.
- **Conversion clarity:** Use descriptive calls to action and straightforward supporting copy.

These are documented design priorities, not claims of measured conversion improvements.

## UX process and design decisions

### 01 — Customer journey and information architecture

Review the journey from arriving on the homepage to learning about the product, choosing a book, providing personalization details, and progressing to checkout. Prioritize clear labels and predictable next steps.

### 02 — Messaging and visual hierarchy

Develop a hero section that communicates the offer quickly. Support it with concise benefit-led copy, product imagery, and a prominent primary action.

### 03 — Trust-focused product and checkout experience

Treat the product page and checkout as a single customer journey. Pay particular attention to the legibility of pricing and shipping details, the placement of personalization steps, form clarity, and consistent visual cues.

### 04 — Responsive interface refinement

Adjust spacing, section order, typography, and call-to-action prominence for mobile browsing. Maintain a warm, premium, and giftable visual identity.

### 05 — Iteration and quality review

Review the published experience for readability, content consistency, and friction points. Use feedback to guide improvements rather than relying only on visual preference.

## AI-assisted design workflow

AI tools were incorporated into ideation and production workflows, including exploration with **Gemini**, **Gamma**, and other generative design/copy tools. The project also explored **AI-agent-assisted website production and iteration**.

AI assistance supported drafting, layout exploration, analysis, and iteration. Design judgment, brand consistency, and customer-facing review remained essential. This README does not claim that autonomous agents replaced usability research or that AI-generated results were independently validated.
